# Logic Mine — External Market Evidence Watch

**Status: RESEARCH / NOT_YET_VERIFIED**

This repository tracks the external capability/skills ecosystem relevant to future logic-product packaging.

## Evidence anchors

1. Google ARD — publishing, discovering, and verifying AI capabilities.
   https://developers.googleblog.com/announcing-the-agentic-resource-discovery-specification/

2. Google Cloud Agent Registry — standalone skills, semantic search, revisions, governance.
   https://docs.cloud.google.com/agent-registry/search-agents-and-tools

3. Google Research — Agent Skills lifecycle and marketplace/registry dynamics.
   https://research.google/pubs/a-survey-on-agent-skills-externalized-procedural-knowledge-in-language-models/

4. Microsoft Research — SkillWiki and continuous skill evolution.
   https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/skillwiki-a-living-knowledge-infrastructure-for-agent-skills/

5. Internet of Agents — capability discovery and continual discovery.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.19113

## Working distinction

A **Skill** is not automatically a **Logic Architecture**.

The research question is whether a deeper class of reusable capability can be produced by continuous logic extraction/reconstruction and then exposed to future agent systems as a product.

No uniqueness or market-size claim is inferred from absence of competitors in a search.
